# AI Usage Acknowledgement (Deliverable 2)

For Deliverable 2, AI tools were used as a support aid during analysis, testing, and documentation.  
AI was not used as an autonomous decision-maker; instead, it was used to accelerate drafting and review tasks while final responsibility remained with the team.

## Scope of AI Use

AI assistance was used to:
- brainstorm edge cases and wrong-input scenarios for robustness testing,
- suggest additional negative test ideas (validation failures, authorization failures, state-transition errors),
- help identify potential requirement-coverage gaps between scripts, tests, and report content,
- propose wording improvements for report sections and checklists,
- improve documentation clarity (structure, consistency, and traceability formatting).

Examples of AI-assisted areas in this milestone:
- robustness discussion around invalid inputs and corner-case behavior,
- checklist-style mapping of rubric requirements to repository evidence,
- wording improvements for architecture/pattern explanations,
- review prompts for consistency between diagrams and implementation notes.

## Human Oversight and Validation

All final code, tests, diagrams, and documentation were reviewed by team members before inclusion.
Any AI suggestions were treated as draft guidance and were accepted only after manual verification against:
- source code behavior,
- test outcomes,
- milestone requirements and rubric criteria.

## Advantages
- Faster discovery of potential edge cases and robustness scenarios.
- More efficient drafting of structured report sections and requirement matrices.
- Helpful second-pass review perspective for consistency and completeness checks.
- Reduced time spent on repetitive documentation formatting tasks.

## Limitations / Risks
- AI suggestions may be incorrect, incomplete, or overly generic.
- AI may produce plausible wording that does not precisely match implementation details.
- Over-reliance could weaken technical precision if outputs are not independently validated.

## Mitigation Measures
- Manual verification of all AI-assisted outputs against actual implementation artifacts.
- Preference for repository evidence (code/tests/scripts) over generated claims.
- Iterative review to align documentation with observed system behavior.
- Explicit retention of human authorship and approval for final submitted content.
